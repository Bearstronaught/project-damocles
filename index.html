      The wreck

        Deep scans

        When every target they can reach is done, scouts scan the whole system from its edge. It never runs out, earns steady XP, and now and then turns up credits or wreck alloy.

        Contracts board

        Contracts never expire. When one is done, hand it in to be paid: deliveries are handed over from your hold. Finished or discarded, a new one is posted 10 minutes later.
